Privacy Policy — Verago

A language-learning adventure for Kannada, Marathi & Tamil

Last updated: 2026-07-25

Verago ("the app") is built by Kiran Sridhar. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect. Keeping it short on purpose — there isn't much to say, because almost nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

The app has no advertising and no third-party tracking, and it plays fully offline. Unless you create an optional account (next section), your learning progress never leaves your device; the only things that do are the crash reports, update checks and speech recognition described below.

The following data is stored only on your device:

  • Your XP, level, streak, completed locations, and achievements
  • Your onboarding status and settings (vibration toggle, etc.)
  • A list of questions you got wrong, used to populate Review mode
  • Usage counts powering a developer-only Analytics screen — these are never transmitted

You can erase the progress on this device at any time from Settings → Reset All Progress.

Optional account & cloud sync

You can optionally create an account (email + password) from Settings → Sign In to Sync so your progress can follow you across devices. If you do:

  • Your email address and a securely hashed password are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) solely to operate your account. We never see your password, send marketing email, or share your address with anyone.
  • Your game progress (the same XP/streak/locations data listed above) is backed up to your account so another signed-in device can download it.
  • Nothing else is collected — no contacts, no location, no recordings.

You can Sign Out at any time (progress stays on your device), and Delete Account on the same screen permanently erases your account and all cloud-saved data. If you never sign in, no account data exists and the app remains fully offline.

Microphone & speech recognition

The optional Voice Practice feature listens to you saying a word and scores how close your pronunciation was. To turn speech into text it uses your device's own speech-recognition service — Apple's on iOS, Google's on Android. That means:

  • Your audio is processed by that service, which may transmit it to Apple or Google for transcription, exactly as system dictation does. Their handling is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • The developer never receives your audio or your transcript. The pronunciation score is computed on your device and then discarded.
  • No recording is stored on the device after scoring, and none is ever uploaded to us.

The app requests microphone and speech-recognition permission the first time you open Voice Practice. You can decline or revoke it at any time in your device's system settings; the rest of the app will continue to work normally.

Crash reports and update checks

Two small automatic calls leave the device. Neither contains your name, email, location, or game progress:

  • Crash reports (Sentry). If the app crashes, the error message, the JavaScript stack trace and which component failed are sent to Sentry so the bug can be fixed. The app version, OS version and device model travel with the report. File paths and internal storage keys are stripped out before anything is sent, and no personal identifiers are attached.
  • Update checks (Expo Updates). On cold start the app asks Expo's servers whether a newer JavaScript bundle exists for your installed version. Only the app version and update channel are sent.

Speech playback

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce words. Text sent to the TTS engine is handled entirely by your operating system.

Audio (background music)

The app plays a short, original music loop that ships inside the app bundle. Nothing is streamed.

Children

The app contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no third-party links beyond an optional "Report a Bug" email. It is suitable for all ages. Creating an account is optional and never required to play.
