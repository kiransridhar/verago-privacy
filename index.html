Privacy Policy — Verago

A language-learning adventure for Kannada, Marathi & Tamil

Last updated: 2026-07-13

Verago ("the app") is built by Kiran Sridhar. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect. Keeping it short on purpose — there isn't much to say, because almost nothing leaves your device.

What we collect

By default, nothing is sent to a server. The app has no analytics tracking of you, no advertising, and works entirely offline.

The following data is stored only on your device:

  • Your XP, level, streak, completed locations, and achievements
  • Your onboarding status and settings (vibration toggle, etc.)
  • A list of questions you got wrong, used to populate Practice mode

You can erase all of this at any time from Settings → Reset All Progress.

Optional account & cloud sync

You can optionally create an account (email + password) from Settings → Sign In to Sync so your progress can follow you across devices. If you do:

  • Your email address and a securely hashed password are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) solely to operate your account. We never see your password, send marketing email, or share your address with anyone.
  • Your game progress (the same XP/streak/locations data listed above) is backed up to your account so another signed-in device can download it.
  • Nothing else is collected — no contacts, no location, no recordings.

You can Sign Out at any time (progress stays on your device), and Delete Account on the same screen permanently erases your account and all cloud-saved data. If you never sign in, no account data exists and the app remains fully offline.

Microphone

The Voice Practice feature records you saying a word so you can play it back and compare it against a reference pronunciation. These recordings:

  • Are stored in the app's temporary directory on your device
  • Are never uploaded anywhere
  • Are discarded when you move to the next word or close the app

The app requests microphone permission the first time you open Voice Practice. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's system settings; the rest of the app will continue to work normally.

Speech playback

The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to pronounce words. Text sent to the TTS engine is handled entirely by your operating system.

Audio (background music)

The app plays a short, original music loop that ships inside the app bundle. Nothing is streamed.

Children

The app contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no third-party links beyond an optional "Report a Bug" email. It is suitable for all ages. Creating an account is optional and never required to play.
